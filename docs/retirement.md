Old `better_cooking:*` inventory or placed saved records have **no migration or refund** in this slice; unsupported/unmanaged items keep their native behavior, not newly fabricated paid entitlements.
* Receiving owners removed temporary Better Cooking coexistence guards. Therefore **never assemble/install a mixed generation** containing the old Better Cooking JAR and these source changes.
